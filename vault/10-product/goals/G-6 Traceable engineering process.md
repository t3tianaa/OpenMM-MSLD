# G-6 — Traceable engineering process

Every requirement is traced to features, tasks, code, tests and decisions; every decision is
recorded with its reasons and consequences; knowledge is shared in one versioned place — so that
several humans and many AI agents can work in parallel without losing context or silently
reversing decisions.
